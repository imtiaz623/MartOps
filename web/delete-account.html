Delete your MartOps account or data

You can ask us to delete your MartOps company account, or the data of one manager, at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to imtiazalee02@gmail.com with the subject "Delete MartOps account".
  2. Write your company code and the owner's phone number so we can verify that you are the owner. For a manager, write the manager ID.
  3. We confirm the request and delete the data within 30 days.

What is deleted

Company details, owner and manager accounts, branches and Wi-Fi names, status records, locations and reports. If you connected your own Google Sheet, that sheet belongs to you and is not deleted by us.

What may be kept

Payment records (plan, amount, date and Transaction ID) may be kept as needed for accounting.

See also the Privacy Policy.
