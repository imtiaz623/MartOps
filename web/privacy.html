MartOps Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains what information the MartOps app and its web panel collect, why, and what choices you have. MartOps is developed by Imtiaz Ali Qumbrani (imtiazalee02@gmail.com). Last updated: 26 October 2026.

2. What MartOps does

MartOps is a business app for shop and company owners. The owner adds branches and the Wi-Fi network names of each branch, then adds a manager. The manager logs in once on his Android phone. During the company working time set by the owner, the app records which branch Wi-Fi the phone connects to, when the manager arrived and left, whether the phone was on another Wi-Fi, on mobile data, without internet or switched off, and the phone's GPS position. The owner sees this in the app and on the web panel, with reports and a map link for each position.

3. Information we collect

Account details: company name, owner name, phone number, optional WhatsApp number for reports, manager names and optional phone numbers, and login IDs. Passwords and PINs are stored only in hashed form.

Branch details typed by the owner: branch name, location name, the names of the Wi-Fi networks of each branch, and the company working time (start, end, days).

Status records (manager phones, during company time): the branch whose Wi-Fi the phone connected to, the name of any other Wi-Fi it connected to, mobile data use, no internet, times when the phone was off or the app was not running, and the arrival time, departure time and duration of each status.

Location (manager phones, during company time only): about every 5 minutes and whenever the status changes, the phone's precise GPS position (latitude, longitude and accuracy), the time, whether a mock-location (fake GPS) app was detected, and the times when GPS or the Location permission was switched off. Nothing is recorded outside company time.

Payment details typed by the owner: the plan, the amount and the Transaction ID (TID) of a payment made outside the app. We never ask for card, bank or wallet passwords or PINs.

Optional backup: if the owner pastes a link to his own Google Sheet, status records are also written to that sheet.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, files, messages, call history or advertising ID.

4. Location permission and background use

MartOps asks for precise Location, including "Allow all the time". It is used for two things: reading the name of the connected Wi-Fi network (Android requires the Location permission for this) and saving the manager's GPS position during company time.

On manager phones the app runs a foreground service with a visible notification so that it keeps working when the app is closed or the phone restarts. Outside the company working time the app checks nothing and records nothing.

This record is visible to the owner of the manager's company. Owners must tell their managers that this tracking is used and must follow the laws that apply to them. Managers see a notice in the app before the permission is requested.

5. How we use the information

To show status records, locations and reports to the owner, to run login and subscription (trial, payment verification, expiry), to send daily reports if the owner enables them, to give support, and to keep the service secure. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data is stored with Google (Google Sheets and Google Apps Script) which acts as our service provider. The web panel is hosted on GitHub Pages, which does not store your records.

When the owner taps a map link, the position in that link is opened in Google Maps in his browser or app. If the owner turns on WhatsApp reports, the owner's number and the report text are sent through the WhatsApp Business platform. If the owner connects his own Google Sheet, records are sent to that sheet.

Inside a company, the owner can see all of his managers' records. A manager can see his own records. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Storage and security

Data travels over HTTPS. Passwords and PINs are hashed. Access to records needs a login token. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Retention and deletion

Records are kept while the company account is active. An owner can remove a manager at any time. To delete a company account and its data, or a single person's data, follow the steps at https://imtiaz623.github.io/MartOps/delete-account.html or email imtiazalee02@gmail.com. We delete the data within 30 days. Payment records (plan, amount, date, TID) may be kept as needed for accounting.

9. Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information, and you can withdraw location permission in Android settings. Without Location permission the app cannot record visits or positions.

10. Children

MartOps is made for business use by adults. It is not directed at children under 18.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and the page at https://imtiaz623.github.io/MartOps/privacy.html.
